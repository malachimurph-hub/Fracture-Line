# Credits — FRACTURE LINE (Milestone 2.6, asset branch assets-m26)

- **Game design, code, data, UI and text:** original work made for Malachi Murphy, built with Grok Bot.
- **Factions, units, structures, powers, map and all names:** original. This includes the United States roster names (Halberd, Lynx, Picket, Longreach, Harrier, Wraith, Thunderlance — original names, only inspired by real equipment roles), the maps Varno Crossing, Kessel Pass, Harrow Junction and Ochre Wells, and the TPLA (The Peoples Liberation Army, a working name) and its Laborer, Militia, Rocket Cell, Rattler, Scrapback, Hailstorm, Shrike, Mule and Blight units. No names, art, models, textures, audio or text from any EA or other commercial game are used, extracted or traced.
- **3D models:** original. They are built procedurally in code (`tools/gen_views.gd`, `view/props/prop_meshes.gd`) from low-poly primitives. TPLA salvage tiers are bolt-on parts on the same models.
- **M2.6 model upgrade:** original procedural models in `tools/gfx_m6/core_m6.gd` (EU Wolfhound, Flakwerk, Aegis; TPLA Scrapback, Hailstorm; EU HQ, Heavy Factory, Reactor; TPLA Camp, Den, Workshop) and new detail helpers (rivets, vents, hatches, jerrycans) in `tools/gfx_m3/geo.gd`. Automatic LODs are generated with Godot's mesh simplifier. Surface detail (rain streaks, edge wear, seam and rivet bump, contact shadowing) is original shader code in `view/materials/model_m3.gdshaderinc`.
- **Soldier models and animations:** Quaternius, "Toon Shooter Game Kit" (https://quaternius.com), **CC0 1.0** public domain — recoloured per faction (EU, TPLA, US Marines), re-equipped, decimated and baked to vertex animation (`tools/bake_infantry.gd`).
  - M2.6: re-proportioned (smaller heads, longer limbs, less cartoony) and re-baked. New EU Lancer and TPLA Rocket Cell (Soldier / Enemy plus RocketLauncher) and TPLA Laborer (Enemy plus Shovel, team head wrap), all from the same CC0 kit.
- **Model weathering** (panel lines, chipped paint, rust, grime, base dirt): original shader `view/materials/weathered.gdshader`. The panel, scratch, grime and rust masks and the stencil sheet are generated by `tools/build_textures_m2.py`. The stencils are drawn from geometric primitives, not from a font.
- **Ground decals** (tire tracks, cracks, oil stains, sand drifts, rubble): original and procedural (`tools/build_textures_m2.py`).
- **Textures from ambientCG** (https://ambientcg.com), all licensed **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**. No attribution is required; it is given here anyway.

  M2 uses the 1K PBR sets: colour plus NormalGL, Roughness and Ambient Occlusion. They ship as Texture2DArrays: albedo as ETC2, normal/roughness/AO as ASTC 4x4, both with mipmaps.

- **Sound effects, UI sounds and ambience:** original. They are synthesized from scratch with NumPy DSP (`tools/gen_audio.py`, plus `tools/gen_audio_tpla.py` for the TPLA weapons, rust, salvage, tunnel and trap sounds). No samples or sound libraries are used.
- **Music:** original compositions, synthesized in `tools/gen_music.py`.
- **Voices: AI-generated** (all wording original, `tools/voice_manifest_src.py`). No real person's voice is cloned. All unit voices are male; only the EU and US announcers are female.
  - **Speech model for every newly generated line (EU, US and TPLA units, TPLA announcer): Kokoro-82M** by hexgrad (https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M), **Apache License 2.0** (weights and the built-in voice packs), run with **kokoro-onnx** (MIT) on ONNX Runtime (MIT). Each unit is its own character: a weighted blend of Kokoro's built-in *male* voice packs (`am_*`, `bm_*`, `em_*`, `hm_*`, `im_*`, `pm_*`), its own accent, pitch, pace and grit profile (`tools/voice_cast_m26.py`, generated by `tools/gen_voices_m26.py`).
  - **Accents** are original phoneme-rewrite rules applied to Kokoro's own phonemizer output (Kokoro's bundled eSpeak NG data, GPL-3.0, used as a tool only, nothing of it ships): European first-language rules (German, French, Italian, Polish, Dutch, Swedish, Spanish) in `tools/accent_eu.py`, Southern US for a few US veterans, Arabic and Persian rules in `tools/accent_tts.py`.
  - **TPLA Militia (kept at Malachi's request):** these lines are the shipped M2.5 files, made with **Chatterbox Multilingual** (Resemble AI, MIT) prompted by synthetic accented-English clips from the Piper Persian voices fa_IR amir / ganji / reza_ibrahim. **Licence flag:** those Piper voices are fine-tuned from en_US-lessac, whose training corpus is licensed for research only (see `docs/m2_6/LICENCE_REVIEW.md`). The Militia voice is therefore *not clearly permissive*. A licence-clean Kokoro alternative can be generated with `python3 tools/gen_voices_m26.py --unit tpla_militia --out assets_src/voice_m26_candidates` for side-by-side comparison. Every other TPLA line was regenerated with Kokoro, and no lessac-derived model is used anywhere else.
  - **Processing (offline):** vocal roughness (NumPy amplitude jitter), **Rubber Band** pitch shifting through FFmpeg's `rubberband` filter (librubberband, GPL-2.0, offline tool only), per-character FX chains (saturation, intercom, helmet, oxygen mask, bullhorn, close-mic) and then `tools/import_voice.py`: trim, faction radio filter, two-pass loudness normalization, limiter.
  - **Quality check:** **faster-whisper** (MIT) with OpenAI **Whisper small** weights (MIT), offline. Every line is transcribed. Lines scoring under 0.8 word match are re-taken up to three times with a weaker accent, a slower pace and a cleaner chain. Results are in `tools/voice_qa_m26.json`.
  - **Not used:** Chatterbox (for new lines; too slow on the build machine and its TPLA prompts were lessac-derived), Piper lessac-derived voices, Meta MMS-TTS (CC-BY-NC 4.0), Coqui XTTS-v2 (non-commercial), F5-TTS (CC-BY-NC weights).
  - **Your own recordings:** files in `audio/voice/` always take priority over the AI lines.
- **Tools used offline (not shipped):** FFmpeg (with librubberband), Python with NumPy (BSD), Pillow (HPND), soundfile (BSD), kokoro-onnx, ONNX Runtime, eSpeak NG (via Kokoro's phonemizer), faster-whisper, Godot (headless, for model generation and VAT baking).
