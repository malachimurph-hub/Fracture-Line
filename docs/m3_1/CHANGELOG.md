# Rules of Engagement — M3.1 (versionName 0.3.2, versionCode 16)

## Main menu
- The live animated menu battle is gone, along with its code paths: the background SubViewport,
  the menu-battle setting and the backdrop parking. In its place is a static high-res key-art
  battle scene. It was rendered offline at 4680x2160 from the real in-game models, terrain, VFX
  and sky (US, Germany and Iran vehicles and infantry, explosions and smoke), then downscaled
  to 3120x1440 and saved as a JPEG (`assets/ui/menu_keyart.jpg`). The menu now opens instantly.
  The art is generated by `tests/m31_keyart.tscn`.

## Buildings
- **Pentagon (US Strategic Center)** is bigger: the footprint grew from 3x3 to 4x4 and the model
  is scaled up to match (PS 0.74 -> 0.99), so pathing follows the real size.
- **US HQ** has a rooftop helipad (decorative). It sits on a raised steel deck on legs and has a
  touchdown ring, an edge stripe, a white H, green and amber perimeter lights, a safety-net rim,
  a wind sock and an access stair. The roof clutter (mast, dish, AC, hatch) was moved to make room.

## Building repair
- Select a builder (US Dozer, Germany Engineer Crane, Iran builder) and tap a damaged friendly
  building to repair it. HP comes back gradually: one builder takes about 30 s for a full repair.
  The cost is 25% of the building's price for a full repair, charged as the work goes. The
  builder waits if you run out of supply and stops at full HP. The AI also sends idle builders
  to buildings below 40% HP. Covered by a new test (`resume` suite).

## Units
- **US Longreach is renamed M109A7** in the UI, the Strategic Center description and the docs.
  The two voice lines that said "Longreach" were dropped, so only name-free lines play. The model
  is now a tracked self-propelled howitzer in olive green: low hull with the engine and driver up
  front, a big flat-sided turret with a long bustle, side doors and stowage, a commander's cupola
  with an HMG, and a long 155 mm barrel with a fume extractor and double-baffle muzzle brake
  resting in a travel lock. **The barrel elevates when firing** and settles back afterwards
  (`VehicleView.elevate`).
- **Germany builder** was redesigned as a European mobile site crane and renamed "Engineer
  Crane". It has a 4-axle wheeled carrier, a low cab, a slewing superstructure with an operator
  cab and counterweight, a 3-section telescopic boom over the carrier, a hook block, outriggers
  and a civilian crane livery. Role and stats are unchanged.

## Aircraft (all factions' airfields)
- Jets now taxi from their bay to the hold-short line, enter the runway, back-taxi to the
  threshold, line up, accelerate down the strip and lift off at its far end.
- On return they fly to an approach fix 8 tiles out, then fly a straight final aligned with the
  runway (glide slope in the view), touch down, roll out, taxi back to their bay, and rearm.
- One aircraft on the runway at a time: departures wait at the hold-short line and arrivals
  circle the fix until the runway is free.
- Taxiing and rolling jets are ground targets. Helicopters are unchanged.
- Deterministic (integer sim, id-ordered runway claims). The `m25` air test now checks taxi,
  lift-off from the runway line, aligned landing and the one-at-a-time runway.

## Camera and controls
- **360° camera**: twist two fingers to rotate (Q/E on keyboard). Pinch zoom and pan still work.
  A small compass on the top bar shows north; tap it to turn back to north-up. Pan, edge pan,
  keyboard pan, minimap view box, selection and HUD arrows all follow the rotation, because they
  use camera ray casts.
- **Two-finger hold select**: hold two fingers still for about 0.35 s and a selection box appears
  between them. Spread or pinch to resize it, then lift to select your combat units inside (same
  filter as drag-select). A twist or pinch before the threshold stays camera control.
- The pause menu has a new **Controls** help panel listing every gesture.

## Graphics ("INSANE" overhaul)
- **Ultra graphics preset, now the default.** Settings > Graphics offers Ultra / High / Medium /
  Low:
  - Ultra: render scale 0.8, 2x MSAA, 4096 shadow map, 16x anisotropic filtering, plus a second
    detail-normal octave.
  - High: 0.75, 2x MSAA, 2048 shadows, 8x anisotropic.
  - Medium and Low: no MSAA, lower scale and anisotropy.
- **Model textures went from 512 to 2048 px.**
  - The panel-line channel used to be a regular 128 px grid, which caused the "visible squares".
    It now has about 120 irregular plates per tile with thin anti-aliased seams, bevels, access
    hatches and rivet rows.
  - There is a new 2048 px **detail normal map** (seams, rivets, hatches, plate steps, grain),
    sampled object-space triplanar with whiteout blending.
  - Grime, scratches and rust masks are smooth periodic noise, so there is no tile seam or grid.
  - The rust colour texture is now 1024 px.
  - All model textures use anisotropic mipmapped filtering.
- **Removed the derivative-based shader tricks.** The `dFdx`/`fwidth` bump, edge-wear and edge
  highlight drew 2x2-pixel blocks on mobile GPUs; the sampled normal map replaces them.
- **Camo fixes**:
  - The US camo patterns read the panel channel, so old grid lines showed through as light
    lines. They now use only noise.
  - The EU splinter camo uses a sheared lattice, so its shards are slanted instead of
    axis-aligned boxes.
  - Concrete tone variation no longer imprints panel lines.
- **Terrain**: splat weights use a B-spline bicubic lookup, so road, shore and terrain-type edges
  are smooth curves instead of stair-stepped squares (High and above).
