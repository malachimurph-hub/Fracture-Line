# Unit and structure icons: sources and licences

All 131 icons in `ui/icons/` (selection menu, build menu and production queue) are our own
renders of the game's own 3D models, made with `tools/gfx_m33/icons.tscn` / `icons.gd`
(commit c88d824). No third-party art is used, so no CC0 or other licence applies.

| Icons | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ui/icons/*.png (131 files) | Rendered in-engine from our own models (view scenes in `view/`) | Original work, part of this project |

If an icon ever has to come from outside the project, add it here with its source URL, author
and licence (CC0 or similar only).
